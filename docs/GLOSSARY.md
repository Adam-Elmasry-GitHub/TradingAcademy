# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ask** | The lowest price a seller will accept. You buy at the ask. |
| **ATR** | Average True Range: typical price movement per bar. Used for stops and sizing. |
| **Backtest** | Testing exact strategy rules on historical data. |
| **Bid** | The highest price a buyer will pay. You sell at the bid. |
| **Break-even win rate** | Win rate needed to neither win nor lose at a given reward-to-risk: 1 / (1 + R). |
| **Candlestick** | A bar showing open, high, low and close for one period. |
| **CFD** | Contract for difference: a leveraged broker contract mirroring a price. High risk. |
| **Divergence** | Price and an indicator moving in opposite directions; a sign of fading momentum. |
| **Drawdown** | Fall from a peak in account value, in percent. |
| **Edge** | A repeatable reason why a strategy makes money over many trades. |
| **EMA / SMA** | Exponential / simple moving average. |
| **Expectancy** | Average profit per trade: win% x avg win - loss% x avg loss. |
| **FOMO** | Fear of missing out: chasing a move after it already happened. |
| **Kelly criterion** | Formula for the growth-optimal bet size; use a fraction of it. |
| **Leverage** | Controlling a position larger than your deposit; multiplies gains and losses. |
| **Limit order** | Order that fills only at your price or better. |
| **Long / short** | Long profits when price rises; short profits when price falls. |
| **MACD** | 12 EMA minus 26 EMA, with a 9 EMA signal line. |
| **Market order** | Order that fills immediately at the best available price. |
| **Overfitting** | Tuning a strategy to past noise so it fails on new data. |
| **P/E ratio** | Share price divided by earnings per share. |
| **Paper trading** | Trading with simulated money. |
| **Position sizing** | Choosing how many units to trade so the loss at your stop equals your planned risk. |
| **Profit factor** | Gross profit divided by gross loss. Above 1 is profitable; 1.5+ is good. |
| **R / R-multiple** | Your risk on a trade = 1R. A +2R trade made twice the risk. |
| **RSI** | Relative Strength Index, 0-100 momentum oscillator. |
| **Sharpe ratio** | Return divided by volatility; return per unit of risk. |
| **Slippage** | Difference between expected price and actual fill. |
| **Spread** | Ask minus bid. A cost on every trade. |
| **Stop-loss** | Order that closes a trade when price reaches the level that proves you wrong. |
| **Support / resistance** | Price zones where buying / selling has repeatedly appeared. |
| **Take-profit** | Order that closes a winning trade at your target. |
| **Trend** | Uptrend = higher highs and higher lows; downtrend = lower highs and lower lows. |
| **Volatility** | How much price moves. Higher volatility means smaller position size. |
