# Sources and reading list

Everything here is a well-known, widely used resource. Items are grouped by roadmap level and marked:
🟢 free · 📕 book · 🎓 course · 🛠 tool. Read in roughly this order; you do not need all of them.

## Books by level

### Level 0-1 · Foundations and charts
- 📕 John J. Murphy, *Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets*: the standard textbook. Use it as a reference all the way to Level 2.
- 📕 Steve Nison, *Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques*
- 📕 Al Brooks, *Trading Price Action Trends*: advanced; read after you know the basics.
- 📕 Anna Coulling, *A Complete Guide to Volume Price Analysis*

### Level 2 · Technical analysis
- 📕 J. Welles Wilder, *New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems*: original RSI and ATR.
- 📕 John Bollinger, *Bollinger on Bollinger Bands*
- 📕 Thomas Bulkowski, *Encyclopedia of Chart Patterns*: statistics on how often patterns actually work.
- 📕 David Aronson, *Evidence-Based Technical Analysis*: how to test whether TA works at all.
- 📕 Perry Kaufman, *Trading Systems and Methods*: encyclopedic reference.

### Level 3 · Risk management
- 📕 Van K. Tharp, *Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom*: R-multiples, expectancy, position sizing.
- 📕 Curtis Faith, *Way of the Turtle*: a famous rule-based trend system with volatility sizing.
- 📕 Ralph Vince, *The Mathematics of Money Management*
- 📕 Edward O. Thorp, *A Man for All Markets* and William Poundstone, *Fortune's Formula*: the Kelly criterion.

### Level 4 · Fundamentals and macro
- 📕 Benjamin Graham, *The Intelligent Investor*
- 📕 William O'Neil, *How to Make Money in Stocks* (CAN SLIM)
- 📕 Mark Minervini, *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*

### Level 5 · Psychology
- 📕 Mark Douglas, *Trading in the Zone*: the most recommended trading psychology book.
- 📕 Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow*: the science of biases.
- 📕 Annie Duke, *Thinking in Bets*: separating decisions from outcomes.
- 📕 Brett Steenbarger, *The Daily Trading Coach*
- 📕 Alexander Elder, *The New Trading for a Living*: psychology, method and money management in one book.

### Level 6 · Strategy development and backtesting
- 📕 Andreas Clenow, *Following the Trend*
- 📕 Robert Carver, *Systematic Trading* and *Leveraged Trading*
- 📕 Ernest P. Chan, *Quantitative Trading* and *Algorithmic Trading*
- 📕 Marcos López de Prado, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*: overfitting and proper testing (advanced).

### Level 7 · Professional
- 📕 Larry Harris, *Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure for Practitioners*
- 📕 Lawrence G. McMillan, *Options as a Strategic Investment*
- 📕 Sheldon Natenberg, *Option Volatility and Pricing*
- 📕 Stefan Jansen, *Machine Learning for Algorithmic Trading*
- 📕 Jack D. Schwager, *Market Wizards* series: interviews with top traders.
- 📕 Edwin Lefèvre, *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator*: a classic about trader psychology.

## A note on what to avoid

- Courses or 'signal groups' promising fixed monthly returns. Real professionals measure returns per year and talk about drawdowns.
- Any unlicensed broker or 'account manager' contacting you first. Check Finanstilsynet's warning list.
- Heavy leverage, CFDs and short-dated options before Level 6. Regulators (ESMA, Finanstilsynet) publish data showing most retail CFD accounts lose money.
